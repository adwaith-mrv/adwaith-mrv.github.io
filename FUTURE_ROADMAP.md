# Portfolio Development Roadmap

This document serves as a reminder and plan of action for future enhancements to Adwaith V's React Portfolio. These ideas were proposed on **June 1, 2026**, following the successful deployment of the tagline and Resume Button upgrades.

---

## 🌟 Feature 1: Product Showcase Carousel
*   **Concept**: Add an interactive, high-end product carousel.
*   **Inspiration**: Lando Norris's website style/carousel.
*   **Functional Goal**: Clicking a product card in the carousel will route the user to a dedicated, detailed page focusing on the Product Analysis write-up.
*   **Design Tokens**: High visual aesthetics, smooth gliding transition, premium gaming-inspired hover effects.

---

## 📅 Feature 2: Sleeker Experience Timeline
*   **Concept**: Revamp the Experience Timeline section to be visually sharper, more modern, and more readable.
*   **Functional Goal**:
    *   Sleeker vertical tracks and timeline nodes.
    *   Micro-interactions when hovering over career milestones.
    *   Streamlined descriptions with expandable text options or interactive tags for key deliverables (e.g., Salesforce, SME Lending CoE).

---

## 🧭 Feature 3: Dynamic Morphing Navigation Toolbar
*   **Concept**: A futuristic, smooth-floating router toolbar that helps navigation between sections.
*   **Functional Goal**:
    *   **Trigger**: Becomes populated immediately when the user clicks the **"Know More"** lock-breaker button on the Hero screen.
    *   **Animation**: Physically transitions/floats from the middle of the screen to the top-right corner of the viewport during the initial scroll down to the "About Me" section.
    *   **Behavior**: Locks at the top right as a sticky navbar. Includes smooth anchor routing to easily scroll to any target section (About, Experience, Certifications, Skills, Contact).
